About The Project

Project Overview

The ResearchIQ is an intelligent web-based application designed to assist students, researchers, and academicians in analyzing research papers efficiently. The system enables users to upload research papers in PDF format, automatically extract textual content, generate concise summaries, identify significant keywords, and maintain a searchable analysis history. This application reduces the time required to review lengthy research documents and improves research productivity.

Problem Statement

Researchers and students often spend considerable time reading and understanding large research papers. Identifying the core contribution, methodology, and key concepts manually can be time-consuming. The proposed system addresses this challenge by providing automated document analysis, enabling users to quickly understand the essential information contained within a research paper.

Project Objectives

  • Develop a web-based platform for research paper analysis.
  • Extract textual content from uploaded PDF documents.
  • Generate concise summaries of research papers.
  • Identify and display important keywords.
  • Store analysis history using a database system.
  • Provide downloadable reports for future reference.
  • Improve the efficiency of academic research activities.

By generating summaries, identifying keywords, and maintaining historical records, the system enhances research efficiency and serves as a valuable academic tool for students, researchers, and educational institutions.
